Training Plan

Upper / Lower + 2× climbing + 2× abs

Rules
EffortStop with 1–2 reps left (Thu: 2–3)
ProgressionHit the top of the rep range on all sets → add weight → back to the bottom
RestCompound lifts 2–3 min, isolation 1–1.5 min
DeloadEvery 6–8 weeks: one week at half the sets
Missed climbRest. Don't add volume
Too tiredDrop Thu, move Bulgarian split squat to Mon
Protein1.6–2.2 g per kg body weight per day
CaloriesSmall surplus, +200–300 kcal
Sleep7–9 h
AbsProgress with slower reps, pauses and harder variations. Visible abs also need low body fat
